{{# The working sample for statamic-inline-edit (v1.11). Every example on this page is one field, or one cell of a grid, that an editor may change on the page itself. Each has a one-line caption that tells a visitor what to do. The fields are in resources/blueprints/collections/pages/inline_edit_demo.yaml and the content in app/Demo/SeedsInlineEdit.php. Plain fields are `{{ editable:handle }}` instead of `{{ handle }}`. That is the whole integration, and for a visitor it renders byte for byte what it rendered before: no wrapper, no script, no attribute. A grid cell by row id, a picture cell with an alt text, and a window over several values are PHP helpers (InlineEdit::cell / popup). The small `ie_demo` tag in app/Tags/IeDemo.php hands them to this template; a real site calls them from its own components. #}}

This page shows every kind of field Statamic Inline Edit can edit. Sign in to the control panel, then press Edit page in the corner (or Ctrl/Cmd + Shift + E). Every editable thing gets a dashed outline. Double-click it, or focus it with Tab and press Enter. Esc discards a field, Ctrl/Cmd + S saves everything. Not signed in? You are seeing exactly what a visitor sees: plain HTML, no script.
